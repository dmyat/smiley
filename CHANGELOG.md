# Release notes

What changed in each version of the app, newest first.

## 0.2.0 (12 Nov 2026)

New since 0.1.21:

- **History page on the Perf tab.** Every drag run, dyno pull and brake test is
  listed in plain words, newest first, with all its figures. Each entry is kept
  for 30 days and then deleted automatically.
- **Warm-up light.** Top right on every tab, from the engine oil temperature:
  blue "ENGINE COLD", amber "WARMING UP", green "WARMED UP".
- **Instagram row on the Info tab.** It replaces the car Wi-Fi address row,
  which only served the Phone tab that is switched off.
- The corner label is back to the app name, maker and version only.

## 0.1.18 to 0.1.21

- The app is listed as PERFORMANCE in the car's menu.
- The Instagram handle was tried in the corner label (moved to Info in 0.2.0).

## 0.1.17

- Fixes from the first on-car video of 0.1.16: Info asks the engine computer
  for the VIN and type again if the first read is missed, and the controller's
  focus box is hidden after a finger tap on a tab.

## 0.1.16

- The Christmas snow and surprises move only on the GPU renderer, so the Basic
  renderer is not slowed down.

## 0.1.15

- On-screen instructions say "this app" instead of the app's name.

## 0.1.12 to 0.1.14

- Live Stats: signal bars for the fast readings (RPM, boost, boost target,
  load, torque), dials for the rest, and blue-to-red dials for temperatures.
- Thicker, sportier dials with MIN and MAX beside each one.
- A setting to choose all dials, all bars, or the mix.

## 0.1.10

- Info shows the hardware and software numbers from the BMW parts list.
- The Tune row says plainly what was found and how.

## 0.1.6 to 0.1.9

- Christmas Green theme with falling snow, now the default.
- Christmas Red, a second Christmas colour scheme.
- Christmas surprises (Santa's sleigh, a shooting star, a snowman) that play
  only in the empty strip at the bottom, never over a gauge or button.
- Live Stats shows six gauges a page on two pages, and a gauge is changed by
  picking from a list.

## 0.1.5

- Fixed the boost gauge showing no data on the default first page.

## 0.1.3 and 0.1.4

- Phone, Connect and Flash tabs switched off for now.
- Boost is shown against standard air pressure until the car reports its own.
- Fixed boost dropouts and the cut-off message on the Dyno page.
